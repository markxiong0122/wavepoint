# Wavepoint Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 9, 2026

Wavepoint is an iPhone utility that helps you review and remove tracks from your Spotify Liked Songs library.

## Data Wavepoint accesses

When you choose Continue with Spotify, Spotify asks you to authorize access to your account. Wavepoint requests permission to read your saved music and recent listening history, and to remove items from your library only after you confirm a removal batch.

Wavepoint receives Spotify access and refresh tokens needed to make those requests. Tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Your cleanup decisions remain on your device and are not uploaded to a Wavepoint database.

## Data collection and tracking

Wavepoint does not sell personal data, use advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or collect analytics in this version. Wavepoint does not store your Spotify library, listening history, or cleanup decisions on its own servers.

Spotify and Supabase process authentication and API requests under their own terms and privacy policies. Album artwork and available audio preview clips are loaded from Spotify-provided URLs.

## Library changes

Swiping a song does not immediately change your Spotify library. Songs marked for removal are staged on your device. Wavepoint sends a removal request to Spotify only after you review the staged list and confirm the exact batch count.

## Retention and deletion

Wavepoint retains Spotify authorization tokens in the iOS Keychain so you can remain signed in. Using Sign Out removes Wavepoint's locally stored Spotify tokens. Deleting the app removes its local app data according to iOS behavior. You can also revoke Wavepoint from your Spotify account's Apps page.

## Children

Wavepoint is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If Wavepoint's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.
