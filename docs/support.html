Wavepoint Support

Wavepoint is a fast, review-first cleanup tool for Spotify and Apple Music.

Spotify

Spotify cleanup and automatic card playback require Spotify Premium and the Spotify app. While Wavepoint remains in Spotify Development Mode, the Spotify account must also be allowlisted by the app owner.

Apple Music

Apple Music requires an active subscription and Sync Library. Wavepoint creates Wavepoint Dumpster 🗑️; open Music and use Delete from Library to complete deletion. Removing a song from the playlist alone does not delete it from the library.

Get help or request privacy deletion

Open a private-data-free support request. Do not include access tokens, screenshots containing account information, or song-library exports. For account deletion, Spotify users can also use Account → Delete Account in the app.
