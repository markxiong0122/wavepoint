Wavepoint Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026. Wavepoint helps you review buried songs in Spotify or Apple Music and safely stage cleanup choices.

Music data

Spotify access is used to read saved music, verify subscription eligibility, control cleanup playback, and remove only a batch the user explicitly confirms. Credentials are kept in secure device storage. Supabase Auth retains the Wavepoint login record and provider identity metadata needed for authentication.

Apple Music library access stays on the device. Confirmed choices create or update Wavepoint Dumpster 🗑️; Wavepoint cannot directly delete songs from the Apple Music library. Apple Music library data is not sent to Wavepoint's Supabase backend.

Track names and identifiers, artists, artwork, listening history, library size, playback history, saved dates, and individual Keep/Remove choices are not sent to analytics or crash reporting.

Anonymous analytics and diagnostics

PostHog receives anonymous Wavepoint flow events such as app open, provider connection, deck load, first decision, review, completion, and abandonment. Properties are limited to provider family and a coarse error category. PostHog also receives an anonymous installation identifier and standard app/device information. Session replay, UI autocapture, person profiles, surveys, and feature flags are disabled.

Firebase Crashlytics receives crash stack traces, relevant application state, app version, device and OS information, and random installation/session identifiers. Non-fatal reports contain only a fixed operational category. Google states that Crashlytics crash data and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal begins. Firebase Analytics is not included.

Wavepoint does not sell personal data, use advertising SDKs, or track people across other companies' apps or websites.

Service operation

Spotify, Apple, Supabase, PostHog, and Google process data under their own policies. Wavepoint's Supabase functions log only a random request ID, function name, and response status—never authorization headers, provider tokens, user IDs, request bodies, or music data.

Library safety

Swiping never immediately changes a library. Spotify removals are staged locally until review and confirmation. Apple Music choices are added to the Dumpster only after confirmation; the user completes any deletion in Music.

Retention and deletion

Spotify users can use Account → Delete Account to delete the Supabase authentication record and clear local credentials. Sign Out clears local Spotify credentials and signs out of Supabase. Apple Music does not create a Wavepoint server account. Deleting Wavepoint does not delete a Spotify account or songs and does not automatically revoke Spotify authorization.

Analytics and diagnostics follow the configured PostHog retention and Crashlytics retention described above. Submit privacy deletion requests through Support.

Children

Wavepoint is not directed to children.
