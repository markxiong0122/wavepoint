Wavepoint Privacy Policy

Effective July 9, 2026. Wavepoint is an iPhone utility that helps you review and remove tracks from your Spotify Liked Songs library.

Data Wavepoint accesses

With your permission, Wavepoint reads your saved music and recent listening history, removes library items only after you confirm a removal batch, and controls Spotify playback during an active cleanup session. After login and library loading, Wavepoint may open Spotify automatically with the first cleanup track, then play 15-second segments as each new card appears. Spotify access and refresh tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Cleanup decisions stay on your device.

Supabase Auth stores a Wavepoint authentication record so you can remain signed in. It includes a Supabase user ID and Spotify-provided account metadata such as your Spotify account ID, email address, display name, and profile image URL when Spotify supplies them. Wavepoint uses this information only for authentication and account security.

Collection and tracking

Wavepoint does not sell personal data, use advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or collect product analytics in this version. It does not store your Spotify library, listening history, cleanup decisions, or playback history in a Wavepoint database. Spotify and Supabase process authentication and API requests under their own policies, and their infrastructure may process standard request information for security and service operation.

Library safety

Swiping never immediately changes Spotify. Removals are staged locally and sent to Spotify only after you review the list and confirm its exact count.

Retention and deletion

Supabase retains your Wavepoint authentication record until you delete the account. In Wavepoint, open Account. Sign Out clears locally stored Spotify tokens and signs out of Supabase. Delete Account permanently deletes the Supabase authentication record and clears local Spotify credentials after a second confirmation.

Deleting a Wavepoint account does not delete your Spotify account or songs and does not automatically revoke Spotify's authorization grant. You can separately revoke Wavepoint from your Spotify account's Apps page. Because iOS Keychain items can outlive an app installation, use Sign Out or Delete Account before uninstalling if you want credentials cleared immediately.
