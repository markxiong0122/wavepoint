Wavepoint Privacy Policy

Effective July 9, 2026. Wavepoint is an iPhone utility that helps you review and remove tracks from your Spotify Liked Songs library.

Data Wavepoint accesses

With your permission, Wavepoint reads your saved music and recent listening history, and removes library items only after you confirm a removal batch. Spotify access and refresh tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Cleanup decisions stay on your device.

Collection and tracking

Wavepoint does not sell personal data, use advertising SDKs, track you across apps or websites, or collect analytics in this version. It does not store your Spotify library, listening history, or cleanup decisions on a Wavepoint server. Spotify and Supabase process authentication and API requests under their own policies.

Library safety

Swiping never immediately changes Spotify. Removals are staged locally and sent to Spotify only after you review the list and confirm its exact count.

Retention and deletion

Sign Out removes Wavepoint's locally stored Spotify tokens. You may also delete the app or revoke Wavepoint from your Spotify account's Apps page.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, open a Wavepoint issue.
